About Cartoon Watcher & Copyright Policy

Committed to authorized open-source and public domain animation streaming.

Cartoon Watcher is a dedicated frontend web application designed to showcase and stream legally authorized animated films, open-source CGI masterpieces from studios like Blender Foundation, public domain classic cartoons, and officially licensed independent shorts.

Our Content Commitment

  • Authorized Embedding Only: We exclusively embed videos using official authorized players (such as YouTube's official iframe API with privacy-enhanced options) provided directly by the creators or copyright holders.
  • No Piracy or DRM Bypassing: We never host pirated video files, bypass paywalls, circumvent digital rights management (DRM), or scrape private video URLs.
  • Attribution & Licensing: Every entry in our catalog includes studio credits, verified license types (e.g., Creative Commons, Public Domain, Authorized Creator Permission), and direct links to original sources.

Local Data Storage

Your watchlist and viewing history are stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device and is restricted to your current browser profile. No user accounts or backend tracking are required.

Content Removal Requests

If you are a copyright holder or creator and wish to update attribution, modify embed parameters, or request the removal of a title from our index, please reach out via our repository maintainers on GitHub. We promptly honor all creator and rights-holder requests.
